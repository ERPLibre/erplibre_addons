ERPLibre — Stream Deck

The inventory of the employees' Stream Decks. Each workstation that runs the GNOME extension ERPLibre Home enrolls with this database, then reports its state and its deck events here: which decks are plugged in, since when, which program drives them and whether their service runs. Odoo never opens a connection to a workstation.

Odoo also holds the deck settings it wants on each workstation: the deck program, the plugins turned off, the session refresh, the games and the layout of the home keys of each deck model. The workstation receives them in the reply to its report, and its employee applies them with a click (see Configuration).

The inventory belongs to its employee; a manager sees the whole fleet. Nobody writes it by hand: only the workstation routes write it.

A workstation shows what its last report said: operating system, GNOME Shell, extension and contract versions, branch and commit of the ERPLibre checkout, deck program and the state of its service since when, its decks and its events. Its employee may rename it and revoke its token; the manager may also archive it. Fleet › Workstations adds the column Silent since and the filter Silent. The filters Configuration pending and Configuration refused select the workstations by the state of their configuration, and the tab Configuration of a workstation shows its profile, its effective settings with the layer and the lock of each, its overrides, the key layout of each connected deck, its own key layouts and the state of the revision offered to it.

A workstation and a deck each carry their thread, where the monitoring posts its activities and notes. Every list of events names each event by its label: the state of the service for a service change (Service started, failed, stopped, absent), the kind for any other; the history filters on each label.

Enrolling a workstation

In the extension preferences, page Odoo inventory, add this database by its URL and database name, then choose one of three methods:

  • Pairing code: My workstations › Add a workstation shows a code of 8 characters, valid 10 minutes, once, for the employee who asked for it.
  • API key of the employee (Preferences › Account Security).
  • Login and password of the employee. An account whose RPC access is limited to API keys, as with two-factor authentication, uses one of the two other methods.

The key or the password serves the enrollment only and is kept nowhere. Enrollment returns a workstation token, which the extension keeps in the GNOME keyring; Odoo keeps only its SHA-256. Enrolling the same workstation again keeps its record and replaces its token. Repeated wrong credentials from one address are put on Odoo's login cooldown.

Access

Group Workstations Decks Events
User (every internal user) their own: read, rename, revoke the token, pick a selectable profile, override the free settings those their workstations hold or have held, read-only those of their workstations, and those of a deck they hold or have held, from their first holding on
Manager all: read, rename, archive, revoke the token, assign any profile, override and lock settings all, read-only all, read-only

A deck lent to another employee follows the workstation that holds it. Each employee reads the history of a deck from the moment one of their workstations first held it: when the deck was created on it, or when a Changed workstation event moved it there. Odoo dates that moment itself, never with a time the workstation sent, and keeps it when the deck comes back after a loan. The lender therefore still reads the deck and its whole history from their first holding on, the events at the other workstation included: each event carries a copy of the name of its workstation and of its employee, so the lender never needs, nor gets, the other workstation's record. The borrower reads nothing from before its arrival. A workstation that only sends an event carrying the serial number of a deck held elsewhere reads that event and the deck's name on it, never the deck's record nor its history.

When a workstation reports a deck that another workstation holds, the deck moves to it without confirmation, and Odoo adds an event Changed workstation (moved) dated at the reception. It names the new workstation and its employee, and the workstation the deck left, each by a copy of its name. Its employee becomes a holder of the deck from that moment, unless they held it before; the employee of the former workstation keeps reading the deck and its history, even when that workstation never sent an event of it. The same workstation reporting the deck again moves nothing.

Archiving a user archives their workstations and revokes their tokens; the workstations, their decks and their events stay readable by the manager. Pairing codes are readable by nobody.

The configuration layers. A manager creates and changes the profiles, the deck models and every settings line, and alone sets a lock. An employee reads the selectable profiles, the profile of each of their workstations and every deck-model layer, which hold nothing personal; never a profile assigned only to another employee's workstation, nor the overrides of another employee. On their own workstations they create, change and delete the overrides of the settings that no layer locks, never Locked nor Source. They pick a selectable profile, in place of no profile or of another selectable one only: a profile the manager assigned without making it selectable stays, and an employee never clears a profile. The state of the configuration (revisions, offered and applied values, state, reason, first-pilot choice) is written by the report route only.

The key layouts follow the same rules, the lock being on a whole layout: an employee reads the layouts of the layers they read, and on their own workstations creates, changes and deletes the layouts, and their keys, of the deck models that no layer locks, never Locked, Source nor Kept in the workstation's file. Only the report route adds to the catalog. A plugin's id is the name of its file, and the user's own plugin directory holds files of their choosing: an employee reads the plugin:<id> actions their own workstations offer, never those only another employee's offer, and names no other in a key; the rest of the catalog is read by everyone.

Monitoring

Odoo watches four problems: a deck service that stays failed, a silent workstation, a deck unplugged for long and a deck that changed workstation. It always writes the history. It opens Odoo activities only when Settings › Stream Deck enables them, which is off by default.

History, always

Event Written by When
Service started, failed, stopped, absent (service_changed) the workstation its deck service changes state
Workstation silent (silent) Odoo the workstation has sent no report for the silent delay; dated the end of that delay after its last report
Workstation heard again (heard_again) Odoo the first report after a silence; dated its reception
Changed workstation (moved) Odoo a workstation reports a deck that another one holds (see Access)

A deck unplugged for long has no event of its own: its unplugged event already dates it.

The cron Stream Deck: watch workstations and decks runs every 5 minutes, which is the granularity of every delay. It marks a workstation silent once (Silent since); its next report clears the mark, and so do archiving it and removing its token. A workstation that is archived, has no token (unenrolled or revoked) or has never reported never goes silent. The extension reports every 15 minutes and not while the screen is locked: a workstation turned off for the night, asleep or locked beyond the delay goes silent, then is heard again at its next report.

Activities, on demand

Problem Activity type, on Opens Closes
Deck service failed Deck service failed, on the workstation the service has stayed failed for its delay by itself, at the report that carries another state
Workstation silent Workstation silent, on the workstation the workstation goes silent by itself, at its next report

  • Recipient. Employee: the employee of the workstation, or of the workstation that holds the deck; for a move, the new holder, the lender reads the move in the history. Manager, the default: the user named in the Settings. Employee and manager: one activity for each.
  • Never a duplicate. A record has at most one open activity of a type per assigned user. A deck with an open Deck changed workstation activity opens no other at a new move, even for another holder: the move is posted as a note to the deck's thread. A Deck service failed or Deck unplugged for long activity marked done while its problem lasts opens again at the next run of the cron; a silence opens its activity once.
  • Closing. A closed activity stays done, with the feedback « Resolved at : », the time in the time zone of the assigned user (UTC without one) and the text in their language. Archiving a workstation or its employee, or revoking its token, closes its activities and the Deck unplugged for long activities of its decks with « the workstation is archived » or « the workstation is unenrolled ». Archiving a user, from the Settings or by their departure, closes the activities still assigned to them, Deck changed workstation included, with « the assigned user is archived », where Odoo would delete them. Closing does not depend on the Settings: an activity opened before they were disabled still closes.
  • Archived manager. When the named manager is archived later, only the employee part opens, and each run of the cron writes a warning to the log, which names nobody.
  • Access. An activity is read with its record. An employee reads the activities of their workstations, the manager's included, and those of the decks they hold or held, from their first holding on, plus those assigned to them; never those of another employee's workstation. The rest of a deck's thread, its messages and attached files, is read from the first holding on as well, the message that closes an activity counting from the opening of that activity. On a workstation's thread, the employee posts, edits their own messages and closes their own activities; only a manager edits, closes or deletes those of others. Only the monitoring creates activities of its four types.
  • Notification. Odoo tells the assigned user of a new activity as it does for any activity (inbox or e-mail, by their preference). The module sends no e-mail and no notification of its own. Such an e-mail goes through Odoo's mail queue: neither a report nor the cron waits on the mail server.

The silent delay also dates the Workstation silent event, so it holds with the activities disabled. The 72 hours cover a weekend without an alert for a laptop taken home; the 10 minutes let the deck service restart by itself after a brief failure.

What stays history only

  • Every problem while the activities are disabled, the default.
  • A problem whose box is cleared.
  • A deck service failed, or a deck unplugged, for less than its delay.
  • Nothing leaves Odoo: no e-mail, push notification or message beyond Odoo activities. The delays are global, never per workstation or per employee, and the health of the deck itself (firmware, keys) is not watched.

Configuration

Odoo manages a closed list of four settings, each one line of the workstation's ~/.config/erplibre-home/streamdeck.conf:

Setting Key on the wire Line of the file Values Restarts the deck service
Deck program deck_program DECK_PROGRAM tiler, controller, none yes
Plugins turned off plugins_off DECK_PLUGINS_OFF at most 64 plugin ids [a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,63}, joined by commas yes
Session refresh (seconds) session_refresh_sec DECK_SESSION_REFRESH_SEC a number from 0.5 to 60 yes
Games games GAMES on, off no

No path, no command and no plugin to turn on: Odoo cannot make a workstation run anything. A value is typed bare in a line (a word, plugin ids separated by commas, a number) and stored as JSON; a value outside this list is refused at the write. The tab Configuration lists the plugins the workstation found on its disk.

Layers and locks

A setting takes its value from the last of three layers that sets it:

  1. the profile of the workstation (Configuration › Profiles); an archived profile counts for nothing;
  2. the deck model: the settings of the model of each connected deck (Configuration › Deck models, by USB product id). With decks of several models, their layers apply by ascending product id, the highest winning, and the workstation form warns about it;
  3. the workstation itself: its overrides, in its tab Configuration.

A line marked Locked stops its setting there: a locked profile line ignores the models and the workstation, a locked model line ignores the next models and the workstation. Only a manager sets a lock. A setting that no layer sets stays the employee's, on the workstation, and Odoo never adopts it.

A profile marked Selectable is offered to the employees, who each pick one for their own workstations. The manager assigns any profile to any workstation.

Offer and application

At each report of the pilot, Odoo computes the effective configuration of the workstation. When its SHA-256 differs from the last one offered, the Offered revision grows by one. The reply carries the revision and the extension announces it; nothing changes on the workstation until its employee clicks Apply. The extension then rewrites the managed lines of streamdeck.conf, keeps every other line, and restarts the deck service when a setting that needs it changed and the service is running; a stopped deck service is never started. Its next report says whether the revision was applied or refused, and why.

Configuration Meaning
Unmanaged no layer sets a setting of the workstation, it never reported with protocol 1.1, or its extension does not check this database as the pilot; a revision pending until then is withdrawn
Pending a revision is offered and not applied yet, since Configuration pending since
Applied the workstation applied the offered revision
Refused the workstation refused it; Refusal reason says why: invalid:<key> (invalid:key_layouts for the key layouts), write_failed, restart_failed or unit_missing
Changed locally a setting changed in the file; Odoo offers it again

An extension older than contract 1.3 reports without being piloted, and the form says that it is too old. An extension of contract 1.3 is piloted without the key layouts, which start with contract 1.4 (protocol 1.2).

First pilot

The first time a workstation is piloted, its free settings may hold local values that differ from Odoo's. The Stream Deck manager decides in Stream Deck › Configuration › First pilot, as a Settings administrator does in Settings › Stream Deck:

  • Ask the employee, the default: the extension asks once whether to keep the local values, which then become overrides of the workstation with the source Workstation file, or to take Odoo's. The form says that Odoo waits for this choice and names the settings concerned.
  • Keep local values: Odoo adopts them at once, without asking.
  • Take Odoo's values: nothing is adopted.

A local value that is empty, or already Odoo's, raises no question. Locked settings are always offered for the usual confirmation, whatever the choice. The workstation keeps the answer in First pilot choice; applying a first revision counts as Take Odoo's values.

Local changes

After a revision is applied, a managed setting changed in the file, from the menu TODO › Stream Deck for instance, is handled at the next report:

  • a free setting becomes an override of the workstation, with the source Workstation file, and raises no question: the next revision equals the file, and the extension records it applied silently;
  • a locked setting, or a setting removed from the file or made invalid there, is offered again under a new revision, which the employee confirms as any other.

Key layouts

The home screen of each deck model is laid out in Odoo. A key layout is for one USB product id and belongs to a profile, a deck model or a workstation: the tab Key layouts of each, or Configuration › Key layouts. Its form lists one line per key: the index (0 the top left key, numbered row by row), the action and the icon chosen in the catalog, and a label of at most 16 characters without control character. Create the keys adds an empty line for each key of the model, and the preview shows the grid. An action is the home key of a plugin (plugin:<id>), a key of the dashboard (tiles) or a black key (empty); a key without action is left to the deck program, which places its fixtures and its dashboard there. A plugin can have one home key per layout, and a plugin that the layout does not name gets none: the form warns of the Plugins without a key, among the plugins its own workstation offers for a workstation's layout, else among those that a workstation offers in its last report.

The layouts of a model fold as the settings do, profile < deck model < workstation, and Locked holds the whole layout: a locked layout stops the fold for its model. The home key of a plugin turned off, by Odoo or else by the workstation's file, goes as a black key: the workstation refuses a layout naming a plugin turned off. Only the layouts of the connected decks go to the workstation, in the setting key_layouts of the revision, which is locked when one of them is; the extension writes them to ~/.config/erplibre-home/keymap.json when the employee applies the revision, and keeps there the layouts of the models Odoo does not send. Odoo never takes a layout out of the file: a model that no layer lays out, or no longer does, keeps there the layout last applied to it, and a model the file does not name gets the automatic layout of the deck program. The Configuration tab of the workstation says so for each connected model that Odoo sends no layout.

Each workstation gets the layouts within the catalog of its own report, since it refuses the whole revision for a key its deck program cannot lay out: a model that catalog does not describe gets no layout, a key past its number of keys is left out, an action it lacks goes as a black key, as the deck program would show it, and an icon it lacks is left out. A report without a catalog gets no layout; its settings still go.

The catalog (Configuration › Catalog) is the union of what the workstations report: the actions, the icons and the geometry of each deck model. An item that no workstation reports any more stays, with the date it was last seen, to the hour; an action keeps the workstations whose last report offered it (Offered by). The geometry of a model is a constant of its hardware: a model of the table shipped with the module takes the table's, and a model outside it keeps the geometry first reported. No report changes it, so no workstation decides the keys Odoo lays out for the others.

A layout changed in the file after its revision was applied is handled as a setting, by the SHA-256 of each model's layout (canonical JSON: keys sorted, no space, every character outside ASCII escaped as \uXXXX):

  • a free layout becomes a layout of the workstation marked Kept in the workstation's file, with the source Workstation file, and Odoo sends no layout for that model any more; creating or changing one of its keys sends it again. Odoo does not hold the keys of such a layout, so it takes no lock until then: a lock it could never offer again would let a hand edit pass unnoticed;
  • a locked layout, or one removed from the file, is offered again.

The first-pilot question covers the free layouts as the free settings: a free layout of the file whose hash differs from Odoo's puts key_layouts in ask, unless one of the sent layouts is locked, and keeping the local values adopts it as above. A workstation piloted before protocol 1.2 has made its choice already: a layout then follows the rule of a setting newly managed, offered under a new revision.

Odoo alone writes them, and they open no activity: the state is read on the workstation. The history filters on each of them.

Wire protocol 1.2

The extension's CONTRACT.md describes the same protocol from the workstation side.

The client first sends GET <url>/web/login?db=<db> with a cookie jar to select the database, then each call is a POST of a JSON object, answered by a JSON object, under /streamdeck/v1/. Another HTTP method gets 405. Every body carries "protocol": "1.2" for a workstation that takes in the key layouts, "1.1" for one that takes in the settings only, "1.0" before: an unknown major version is refused, a newer minor version is accepted and its unknown keys ignored. Every reply carries "protocol": "1.2"; an error reply adds error.

Route Authentication Body Reply 200
enroll the method's credentials method (pairing_code, api_key or password), code, key or login + password, fingerprint, hostname workstation_id, token
report Authorization: Bearer <token> state, events, lost_events, config (1.1) accepted (the uuids of the events), heartbeat_s (900), config (1.1, to the pilot of a configured workstation)
unenroll Authorization: Bearer <token> nothing else nothing else; the token is revoked
  • fingerprint: 64 lowercase hexadecimal digits, the HMAC-SHA256 of the machine identifier keyed by the Linux user, the URL and the database; the raw identifier never leaves the workstation.
  • state: host name, operating system, GNOME Shell, extension and contract versions, ERPLibre branch and commit, deck program, service state (active, failed, inactive, absent) and the decks connected now (serial number, USB product id, USB path, firmware). The decks of the workstation missing from the list become disconnected. In 1.1, deck_plugins lists at most 128 ids of the deck plugins found on the workstation's disk; its repeats are dropped. In 1.2, deck_catalog is the catalog of the deck program, {"format": 1, "actions", "icons", "models": {<product id>: {"cols", "rows", "keys"}}}, added to the catalog of Odoo; larger than 32 KiB, or of another format, it is ignored, and an action or an icon of an unknown form is skipped, as is a model whose geometry fits no key (no key, or more keys than cells). The numbers of a geometry are integers from 0 to 256; anything else is a 400 bad_request.
  • events: plugged, unplugged or service_changed, each with its own uuid and the time it was observed. moved, silent, heard_again and the four config_* kinds are written by Odoo only: a report that carries one is a 400 bad_request. A uuid already stored is acknowledged again without change, so a report sent again creates nothing. A time more than 5 minutes ahead of the reception is replaced by the reception time.
  • lost_events: the running total of events the workstation dropped unsent; Odoo keeps the largest total received.
  • config (1.1, ignored in a 1.0 body): {"pilot": false} from a destination that is not the pilot of the workstation, which gets no config back. From the pilot, {"pilot": true, "revision_applied", "current", "result", "choice"}: current the four settings as the file holds them, null when absent or invalid; result the outcome of the last revision, {"revision", "status", "reason", "at"} with status applied or refused; choice the answer to the first-pilot question, keep_local or take_odoo, sent once. A value outside the schema, or an unknown status, reason or choice, is a 400 bad_request; a key outside the four settings is ignored. In 1.2, current.key_layouts maps the product id of each layout of keymap.json to the SHA-256 of that layout, null when the file holds none (at most 128), and the reason invalid:key_layouts is known.
  • config of the reply: {"revision", "hash", "settings", "locked"}, plus ask during a first pilot under Ask the employee. hash is the SHA-256 of the canonical JSON of settings (keys sorted, no space), locked the locked settings, ask the free settings whose local value differs from Odoo's, key_layouts included. In 1.2, settings.key_layouts maps the product id of each connected deck that Odoo lays out, within the catalog of the report, to {"keys": [{"key", "action", "icon", "label"}]}, by ascending key, icon and label only when set, at most 16 models; locked then lists key_layouts when one of these layouts is locked.
  • A deck is known by its serial number, key sn:<serial>; a deck without a readable serial number is known by its workstation, product id and USB path, key nosn:<workstation>:<product>:<path>, and is never merged with a deck of another workstation. A serial outside [A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,64} is a 400 bad_request: having no :, it can never name a deck without serial.
  • Every text of a body holds at most 256 characters and no NUL character, a USB product id is 4 hexadecimal digits, and a deck, in state as in an event, carries a serial number or a USB path; anything else is a 400 bad_request and nothing is written.
Status error Meaning
400 bad_request the body is not JSON, or a value is missing, of the wrong type or too long; nothing is written
400 protocol_unsupported unknown major version
401 credentials_refused wrong key or password, unknown, used or expired pairing code, or login cooldown
401 token_invalid missing, unknown or revoked token
403 user_inactive the employee is archived, or is not a Stream Deck user (a portal account, for instance)

Neither a body, a header nor a reply is logged, and every reply is marked Cache-Control: no-store.
