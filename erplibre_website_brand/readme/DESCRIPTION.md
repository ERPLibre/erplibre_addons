Replaces the « Powered by Odoo » credit, and the variants other modules
append to it (« The #1 Open Source eCommerce », « Create a free website »),
with an ERPLibre signature linking to https://erplibre.ca.

Settings › General Settings › ERPLibre website signature, per website:

- the style, one of eleven (A to K), on a light or a dark background,
  with a live preview;
- the colors: those of the mockups, the website palette (accent from its
  first or second color, text from the footer), or custom accent, text and
  background colors, with an optional background behind the logo
  (transparent by default);
- the logo, the ERPLibre logo by default;
- the phrase before « ERPLibre »; left empty, the default « Built with »
  follows the language of the page (fr_CA: « Créé grâce à »);
- the text of its own that styles B, D, E, F, J and K show besides the
  phrase, one field per style; left empty, its translated default;
- the link, https://erplibre.ca by default.

The phrase and the texts translate per language from the « ERPLibre
signature » tab of the website form (Website › Configuration › Websites).

The fonts of the mockups (Public Sans, JetBrains Mono, Fraunces, SIL OFL
1.1) ship with the module: the signature fetches nothing from a third party.

F, H, I and J show no logo.
