================================
LinkUp — Points and gamification
================================

Applies the LinkUp point scale on top of the Odoo ``gamification`` module.

* A point rule table and a points ledger keep the detail of every award.
* Karma, ranks and badges of ``gamification`` show the balance.
* The native gamification e-mails and challenges are disabled for members.

Status
======

Skeleton: the module installs and declares its dependencies:
``mail_linkup_reflet``,
``gamification``.
Its features are not implemented yet.
